WatchGrapher

Pocket timegrapher · version {{VERSION}} · ESP32-S3

An acoustic timegrapher that fits in your pocket: an ESP32-S3 with a microphone measures the watch's rate, amplitude, beat error and beat rate, and serves the app to your phone or computer over its own Wi-Fi. Timed runs, six-position checks, unattended power-reserve logs, your watch collection — and it shares its data with the WatchGrapher desktop app.

The board needs 8 MB of PSRAM (the "R8" in N16R8): it holds the audio the analysis works on. N8, N16 or R2 boards won't run it.

Before you click Install

  • Plug the board into this computer with a data cable, through the USB-C port labelled USB (it needs no driver) or UART/COM. If the UART port shows up as a CH340 and the install fails to start, use the USB port.
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Safari and Firefox can't flash boards.
  • After flashing you're asked for Wi-Fi details. Enter your home Wi-Fi to make WatchGrapher a device on your network at http://watchgrapher.local; its own hotspot then only comes on when that network is out of reach. Skipped it, or the board was in download mode? Join the hotspot and enter it under Settings → Wi-Fi, or come back here, click Install and choose Change Wi-Fi.

"Failed to initialize" or board not detected? Hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, then click Install. After installing that way, press RST once to start WatchGrapher — until you do, the board waits in its bootloader (the log says waiting for download) and there's no hotspot. If a USB microphone is set as the input, the USB port is busy hosting it: unplug the mic and use download mode as above.

No pickup yet? Settings → Source → Simulated watch runs the whole app on a synthetic watch.

Then

  1. Connect: join the Wi-Fi network WatchGrapher-XXXX (password watchgrapher) and open http://192.168.4.1 — on a phone or any computer. If you entered home Wi-Fi details, http://watchgrapher.local works too.
  2. Change the hotspot password under Settings.
  3. Add your watches, or load your desktop collection.json under Watches.
  4. Measure: pick the watch at the top, put it on the pickup, press Listen.

Updates: on the device, Settings → Check for updates (needs a browser with internet access), or come back here and click Install again — choose not to erase and your watches and logs are kept (settings go back to their defaults). Docs · Source.
